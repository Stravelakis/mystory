## ELI5

Imagine a diary that listens.

You press the big button and talk. The diary writes down every word, in Greek or English. It keeps the diary in a drawer in **your** house (your computer), not in somebody else's house.

Then it helps in three ways:

1. **It puts the pages in order.** You say "this happened the summer we moved", and it puts that page with the other pages from that summer.
2. **It tells you what things are called.** Sometimes people do a thing that has a name, like saying "that never happened" when it did. That one is called *gaslighting*. The diary shows you the name, what it means, and the sentence where you said it.
3. **It helps you write a book.** It puts pages from the same time together and turns them into a chapter. Every sentence points back to the page it came from, so nothing gets made up.

**One rule to know:** to understand your voice, the diary can ask a helper. The helper can be a big company far away (fast, free, but it hears your words) or a helper that lives inside your own computer (nobody else hears anything). You choose. It won't ask the far-away helper until you tick the box that says you understand.

**Put a lock on it first.** Settings → The lock.
